  世界黄金协会数据显示，全球央行购金量2022到2024年连续三年超过1000吨，远超此前十年年均473吨的水平。2026年二季度，全球央行净买入289吨，同比大增62%，创下历史同期最高纪录。


  ## 囤金的不是哪一家，全球都在扫货


  * 波兰：二季度全球央行购金量排第一，买得最凶；

  ## 为什么"越跌越买"？


  普通人买黄金容易追涨杀跌，央行恰恰相反。它不赌短期涨跌，它买的是确定性。


  很多人下意识把央行买金理解成"看好金价要涨"，其实央行从不赌价格。它不择时、不加杠杆、不看下个月的K线，买的是三样东西：外储资产的多元化、对美元体系的风险对冲、信用货币时代最后的底仓。


  今年9月，伦敦金和COMEX黄金单月都跌了6.52%，央行反而把增持量加到了历史最高。在央行的账本里，黄金配置的优先级，高于金价的短期波动。

  地缘冲突、金融制裁、通胀、财政危机——这些能让美元资产缩水的风险，黄金几乎都能扛。


  还有一笔更现实的账：美国联邦债务已经突破40万亿美元，一年光利息就要付超过1万亿。一个国家债务和赤字越滚越大，货币信用就越发存疑，别的央行自然要掂量：手里攥太多美元，到底是财富，还是烫手的风险？


  所以央行集体增持黄金，本质是一场储备的多元化避险。它不是在赌金价涨，是在做资产配置。而恰恰在信用重构的关口，这个价值只会更稳。


  ## 为什么停不下来？


  回到这份最新的公开数据。这一轮增持从2024年11月开始，至今23个月，不是心血来潮，是有清晰逻辑的布局。

  更深一层，这跟官方稳慎推进人民币国际化、优化储备结构是一脉相承的。当越来越多的央行在"减美债、加黄金"时，这份持续23个月的增持，也是在用行动参与这场全球储备格局的重塑。


  而且"越跌越买"还有个易被忽略的效应：因为买盘是长期的、对价格不敏感，等于持续抽走市场上能自由流通的黄金，给金价垫了一块很难砸穿的底。所以金价再怎么震荡，始终跌不透那道由长期买盘筑起来的地板。


  ## 普通人该不该买？


  我知道你憋着这句话。先泼盆冷水：黄金从来不是"稳赚不赔"的投机品，普通家庭买它，不该指望暴富。过去几年金价涨太凶，堆积了大量获利盘，短期波动只会更剧烈，现在盲目进场，大概率是给高位资金抬轿子。

  ## Why "Buy When It Drops"?


  The strategy of "buying when it drops" (越跌越买) is rooted in the belief that market downturns present opportunities to acquire assets at lower prices. Investors often adopt this approach based on several key factors:


  1. **Psychological Factors**: During market declines, some investors may feel anxious or fearful, prompting them to buy low in anticipation of future price recoveries. This behavior can be driven by the desire to capitalize on perceived undervaluation or to avoid missing out on potential rebounds.

  2. **Market Corrections**: A drop in prices may signal a temporary market correction, where overvalued assets adjust to their intrinsic worth. Investors might interpret this as a chance to purchase quality assets at discounted rates, especially if the decline is seen as a result of short-term volatility rather than long-term fundamentals.

  3. **Price Rebound Potential**: Historically, markets have shown a tendency to recover after periods of decline. By buying during a dip, investors aim to position themselves to benefit from subsequent price increases, assuming the underlying value of the asset remains stable or improves.

  4. **Risk Management**: This strategy can help diversify risk by spreading investments across different market conditions. If the market eventually recovers, the lower purchase price can mitigate losses and enhance overall returns.


  However, it is important to note that "buying when it drops" is not without risks. Accurately timing market bottoms is challenging, and prolonged downturns could lead to further losses. The effectiveness of this approach often depends on the investor's ability to distinguish between temporary corrections and sustained bear markets, as well as their risk tolerance and investment goals.


  Ordinary investors tend to buy high and sell low when purchasing gold, but central banks do the opposite. They don't speculate on short-term price fluctuations; instead, they buy with certainty.

They do not time the market, do not use leverage, and do not focus on next month's K-line chart. Instead, they are buying three things: diversification of foreign reserve assets, risk hedging against the dollar system, and the final reserve in the credit money era.

As a country's debt and deficits grow ever larger, the credibility of its currency becomes increasingly questionable, prompting other central banks to consider: is holding too many dollars a source of wealth or a risky liability?


  Therefore, the central banks' collective increase in gold holdings is essentially a diversification of reserves for risk mitigation. It is not a bet on rising gold prices, but rather an asset allocation strategy. And precisely at the critical juncture of credit restructuring, the value of gold will only become more stable.
