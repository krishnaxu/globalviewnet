body_zh: >-
  \    电动自行车CCC认证新规要求严格实施“一车一池一充一码”，生产企业出厂时必须配齐全部必需部件；每辆车出厂都要随车附带一致性证书，内容和车辆登记要求完全匹配。规定型式试验完成后应当保留整车样品，保留时间至证书有效期结束后不少于12个月，实施证后跟踪检查、生产现场或口岸现场抽样检测、市场抽样，切实加严电动自行车证后监督。




  \    目前，市场监管总局正在组织认证机构对相关生产企业开展全面检查。指导督促企业切实提升质量保证能力，确保符合新规要求。检查发现部分电动自行车生产企业存在随意更换关键零部件、生产过程管控不严、例行检验流于形式、预留解除限速篡改空间等问题，对此有关认证机构根据情节轻重，采取了撤销或者暂停相关CCC认证证书的措施，坚决防止不合格产品流入市场。




  　　市场监管总局要求有关生产企业要认真对照新版认证实施规则开展排查，发现不符合要求的，抓紧进行整改。有关认证机构要严格落实新规要求，加强审核把关和证后跟踪检查，切实履行好“守门人”职责，筑牢产品质量安全防线。

  　　核对产品合格证。坚持一车一证，务必索取并仔细核对随车附带的《产品合格证》，确认合格证上的产品型号、整车编码（车架号）、电池品牌型号、充电器型号等关键信息参数与实车完全一致。




  　　留存完整购买凭证。购买时，务必向商家索要正规发票（注明车辆品牌、型号、整车编码等）、产品使用说明书等凭证。建议留存车辆CCC认证证书复印件或电子截图。通过电商平台购买的，需确认商家资质，查看产品评价及售后保障条款。

title_en: New regulations on small electric scooters to take effect on November 1st.
excerpt_en: The State Administration for Market Regulation has comprehensively
  upgraded the Implementation Rules for CCC Certification this year. The new
  regulations for CCC certification concerning three product categories—electric
  bicycles, toys, and strollers—will take effect starting from November 1st.
body_en: >-
  The new CCC certification regulations for electric bicycles strictly require
  the implementation of "one vehicle, one battery, one charger, one code."
  Manufacturers must ensure all essential components are included when producing
  bicycles. Each vehicle must be accompanied by a consistency certificate at the
  time of leaving the factory, with content fully aligned with registration
  requirements. After completing type testing, the entire vehicle sample must be
  retained for no less than 12 months following the expiration of the
  certificate. Post-certification tracking inspections, random sampling at
  production sites or ports, and market sampling will be implemented to
  substantially tighten post-certification supervision of electric bicycles.




  Currently, the State Administration for Market Regulation is organizing certification bodies to conduct comprehensive inspections of relevant production enterprises. It is guiding and urging companies to genuinely enhance their quality assurance capabilities and ensure compliance with the new regulations. During the inspections, some issues were found in electric bicycle manufacturers, including the arbitrary replacement of key components, loose control over the production process, routine inspections conducted as mere formalities, and the creation of opportunities for tampering with speed limit settings. In response, certification bodies have taken measures to revoke or suspend relevant CCC certification certificates based on the severity of the violations, firmly preventing substandard products from entering the market.




  　　The State Administration for Market Regulation requires relevant manufacturing enterprises to conduct thorough inspections in accordance with the new certification implementation rules, promptly carry out rectifications for any non-compliant issues. Relevant certification bodies must strictly implement the new regulations, strengthen pre-certification reviews and post-certification follow-up inspections, effectively fulfill their "gatekeeper" responsibilities, and solidify the product quality and safety defense line.




  　　How to Choose? Pay Attention to Key Information




  　　Select a legitimate business with a good reputation and all necessary permits and licenses to purchase an electric bicycle.




  　　Check the CCC certification mark. When purchasing, ensure the vehicle has obtained CCC certification and that the CCC mark is clearly displayed in a prominent position on the vehicle's body. This serves as the basic proof that the vehicle meets the national mandatory standards.




  　　Verify the Product Qualification Certificate. Adhere to the principle of one certificate per vehicle, and must obtain and carefully check the accompanying "Product Qualification Certificate" to confirm that the key information parameters listed on it, such as product model, vehicle code (VIN), battery brand and model, charger model, etc., are completely consistent with the actual vehicle.




  　　Retain complete purchase receipts. When making a purchase, be sure to obtain an official invoice (stating the vehicle brand, model, whole vehicle code, etc.), product user manual, and other relevant documents. It is recommended to keep copies or electronic screenshots of the vehicle's CCC certification certificate. If purchasing through an e-commerce platform, verify the seller's qualifications, check product reviews, and review the after-sales guarantee terms.




  　　Be vigilant against illegal sales practices. Do not purchase electric bicycles that are sold as new when they are actually expired used vehicles with old labels, or those without new national standard CCC certification, or suspected of having forged certification documents. If you discover such illegal activities, please immediately report them via the 12315 or 12345 hotlines.
